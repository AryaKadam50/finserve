# Income Requirements

## 1. Minimum Income
- The minimum monthly income required to apply for a personal loan is ₹50,000.
- Applications with income strictly below ₹50,000 must be rejected automatically or placed under manual review if other strong compensating factors exist.

## 2. Multipliers
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: The maximum loan amount should generally not exceed 60 times the applicant's monthly income.
- E.g., for an income of ₹50,000, the maximum loan amount is ₹30,00,000.

## 3. Verification
- All stated income must be supported by uploaded documents (e.g., Salary Slip).
- Any discrepancy greater than 5% between declared income and verified income requires manual review.
