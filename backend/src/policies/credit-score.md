# Credit Score Guidelines

## 1. Acceptable Ranges
- **Excellent**: 750 and above. Fast-track approval recommended if DTI is within limits.
- **Good**: 700 - 749. Standard processing.
- **Fair**: 650 - 699. Requires closer scrutiny of employment history and DTI.
- **Poor**: Below 650. High risk of rejection unless exceptional collateral or income is present.

## 2. First-Time Borrowers
- Applicants with no credit history (credit score = 0 or null) are considered "New to Credit" (NTC).
- NTC applicants are limited to a maximum loan amount of ₹5,00,000 and must have a DTI below 30%.
