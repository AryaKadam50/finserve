# Debt-to-Income (DTI) Policy

## 1. Overview
The Debt-to-Income (DTI) ratio is a primary indicator of an applicant's ability to manage monthly payments. It is calculated by dividing total monthly obligations (existing EMIs + proposed EMI) by gross monthly income.

## 2. Standard Thresholds
- **Maximum DTI**: The maximum allowable DTI for standard personal loans is 45% (0.45).
- **High-Income Exceptions**: For applicants with monthly income exceeding ₹1,50,000, a DTI up to 50% (0.50) may be permitted.

## 3. Mismatches and DTI
If the applicant's declared income is significantly higher than the verified extracted income, the DTI must be recalculated using the verified income. If the recalculated DTI exceeds 45%, the application must be flagged for manual review.
